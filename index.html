Uniform & Shop Readiness

Are You Shop Ready?

Every Day. Before You Enter the Shop.
🥽

Eyes

Safety Glasses
  • Clear ANSI Z87-approved safety glasses.
  • Prescription glasses require approved side shields.
  • Safety goggles are acceptable.
👟

Feet

Safety Footwear
  • Steel or composite safety toe.
  • Oil and slip-resistant sole.
  • Black or dark brown.
  • Work shoe or work boot style.
💍

Jewelry

Leave It Off
  • No necklaces, rings, bracelets, or large piercings.

RMCTC Work Shirt

Pants

Work Pants
  • Blue or black work pants.

Hats

Permitted
